Employee & Family Enrollment Guide

Follow four simple steps to select Sagip Buhay Medical Services as your accredited PhilHealth YAKAP clinic and unlock preventive check-ups, lab tests, and covered GAMOT medicines.

Step-by-Step Guide

4 Easy Steps to Register

01

Open eGovPH

Log in to the eGovPH App or PhilHealth Member Portal to verify your PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN).

02

Select YAKAP

Navigate to PhilHealth YAKAP inside the portal and select "Add YAKAP Clinic".

03

Choose Sagip Buhay

Select Sagip Buhay Medical Services (General Trias, Cavite) as your chosen YAKAP provider.

04

Complete FPE & PCU

Meet our medical team during your company's on-site drive or at our clinic for your FPE, PCU verification, and YES form.

What to Bring (Checklist)

  • PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN)
  • eGovPH App

    Installed and verified on your smartphone.

  • Photocopy of 1 Valid Gov't ID

    With 3 specimen signatures.

  • Photocopy of Birth Certificate

    Required only for minor dependents.

Universal Health Care

Sino ang Covered sa PhilHealth YAKAP?

Under the Universal Health Care law, every Filipino is eligible for PhilHealth YAKAP benefits—including employed direct contributors, qualified dependents, senior citizens, PWDs, 4Ps members, and government-subsidized members.

What Happens During Your FPE?

Our medical team performs a quick History Check (medical, family, lifestyle), Vital Signs & Physical Exam (weight, height, BMI, blood pressure), and Risk Assessment so your doctor can recommend any needed screenings or laboratory tests.
