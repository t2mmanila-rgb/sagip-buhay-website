Program 01 • Customized Corporate APE

Tailored to Your Workforce & Budget

We design position-specific Annual Physical Examination packages with preferential corporate group rates and volume discounts based on final test scope and headcount.

01

Physical Assessment

Doctor consultation, medical history review, vital signs, height/weight, and basic fitness-to-work assessment.

02

Laboratory Screening

CBC with platelet count, urinalysis, stool examination (fecalysis), and standard clinical lab panels.

03

Additional Diagnostics

Chest X-ray, ECG, fasting blood sugar (FBS), HbA1C, lipid profile, and other requested tests.

04

Company-Specific Needs

Job-role screenings, medical certificates, and group immunizations (Flu, Hep B, Tetanus, etc.) agreed in your MOA.
